# Literature review index

The updated comparison and gap assessment is [SOTA_NOVELTY_AUDIT.md](SOTA_NOVELTY_AUDIT.md). It reviews the directly relevant work found through 11 November 2026 and identifies why the old calibration/domain-shift project should be retired.

## Most important comparisons

1. **MFEA, Pattern Recognition 2026:** nearest overall paper; multi-cue FEA, MFEA-Bench, and MFEA-CoT LLM-as-judge score. [Paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0031320326004851) · [official repo](https://github.com/syc1336/MFEA-Multi-cue-Facial-Emotion-Recoginition/)
2. **FACR, 2026 preprint:** AU-causal graph and counterfactual faithfulness for facial emotion rationales. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15779)
3. **ReFER, Frontiers 2026:** video FER explanations and label-corruption/anchoring tests. [Paper](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/artificial-intelligence/articles/10.3389/frai.2026.1984257/abstract)
4. **XFER-AU, IEEE Access 2026:** AU-grounded saliency-map evaluation. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3649062)
5. **Right for the Right Reasons?, EMNLP 2026:** causal structure and counterfactual sensitivity of text emotion explanations. [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1382/)
6. **Facial-R1, AAAI 2026:** joint facial emotion recognition, AU recognition, and natural-language reasoning. [AAAI](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/39906)
7. **Emotion-Coherent Reasoning, AAAI 2026:** explanation/prediction coherence with human evaluation. [AAAI](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/37184)
8. **UAR, June 2026:** already overlaps the previous calibration/shift project. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22725)

Do not present the candidate as novel until a final citation search confirms no direct evaluator-audit protocol with visual evidence edits and style controls already exists. See `LITERATURE_SEARCH_LOG.csv` to record the next search pass.
